# Make Product Rows Clearly Clickable

## Goal
Replace the bullet-list appearance in “What We Do” with unmistakable product buttons that communicate each item opens a detail window.

## Changes
- Remove the blue bullet dots and rotated checkmark icons.
- Present every product as a distinct full-width button with a subtle border and contrasting background.
- Add a clear “View details” cue with a familiar open/expand icon on each row.
- Strengthen pressed, focus, and pointer-over states so the interaction is obvious and keyboard accessible.
- Keep the existing product popup, content, images, and admin editing unchanged.
- Ensure labels and action cues fit cleanly on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

## Verification
- Confirm every product button opens the correct popup and closes normally.
- Check keyboard focus and activation.
- Visually verify the three groups at phone, tablet, and desktop widths.
- Confirm the preview builds without errors.
